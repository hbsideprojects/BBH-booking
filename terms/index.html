Terms & Conditions

These Terms & Conditions ("Terms") govern every hire booked through BikeBoxHire.io ("we", "us", "our"). By completing a booking, you ("the Hirer", "you") accept these Terms in full.

1. Acceptance of these Terms

A binding contract is formed between the Hirer and BikeBoxHire.io at the point a booking is confirmed and payment is taken. These Terms, together with our Privacy Policy set out in Part B, form the entire agreement between the parties in respect of the hire.

2. Booking and Payment

The full hire fee (£99.95 for a 7-day hire, or £135 for a 10-day hire) is payable in full at the point of booking. No further sum is payable on collection, save as set out in these Terms in respect of damage, late return, or approved extension.

3. Damage Deposit

A damage deposit of £200 will be authorised and held against the Hirer's payment card at the point of booking. This sum is held as a security authorisation only and does not constitute a charge. The deposit will be released automatically within 5–7 business days of the safe return of the hired equipment ("the Box") in satisfactory condition, subject to Clauses 4 and 5.

4. Damage Schedule

Where the Box is returned damaged, the following charges apply, deducted from the damage deposit referred to in Clause 3:

ItemDescriptionCharge
Locks or hingesBroken, bent, or non-functioning£50
Shell (body)Cracked or split£200 (full deposit)
WheelsMissing or broken£25
HandlesBroken or cracked£100
Interior foamCrushed, torn, or heavily dented£50
GPS trackerMissing or tampered with£50
StrapsTorn or snapped£25

5. Normal Wear and Tear

Normal wear and tear consistent with ordinary use and travel, including minor surface scuffs and light scratching to the exterior shell, is expected and accepted, and will not be charged under Clause 4.

6. Cancellation Policy

  • More than 7 days before the scheduled collection date: full refund of the hire fee.
  • Between 3 and 7 days before the scheduled collection date: 50% refund of the hire fee.
  • Less than 48 hours before the scheduled collection date: no refund of the hire fee.

The damage deposit referred to in Clause 3 is released in full upon cancellation in every case, irrespective of timing.

7. Medical and Injury Exemption

Where the Hirer is unable to travel due to illness or injury, we will, upon provision of a medical certificate or doctor's note, offer at the Hirer's election either a full refund of the hire fee or a rebooking with no time limit, notwithstanding the cancellation windows set out in Clause 6.

8. Late Returns and Extensions

A Hirer wishing to extend their hire must contact us before the scheduled return date. Approved extensions are charged at £15 per day and are subject to availability. Where the Box is not returned by the agreed date and no extension has been approved in advance, a late return fee of £50 per day applies, and will be charged automatically to the payment card on file. The Hirer will be notified via WhatsApp and email in advance of any such charge.

9. Airline Damage

Where the Box is returned with damage clearly attributable to mishandling by an airline, the Hirer must, before leaving the airport, report the matter to the airline and obtain a Property Irregularity Report ("PIR"). The Hirer must provide the PIR to us within 24 hours of return, to hello@bikeboxhire.io. On receipt of a valid PIR, the damage deposit will be released in full and we will pursue recovery from the airline directly. Hirers are strongly advised to hold travel insurance covering sports equipment.

10. GPS Tracking Equipment

Each Box is fitted with a GPS tracking device for the purposes of loss prevention and location monitoring during the hire period. Tampering with, removing, or disabling the tracker will result in forfeiture of the full £200 damage deposit.

11. Collection and Return

Collection and return take place on Tuesdays and Thursdays only, from a partner bike shop in Edinburgh or Glasgow, or from a trusted locker location in Edinburgh, as confirmed at the time of booking. The Hirer is responsible for returning the Box to the same location from which it was collected, on or before the agreed return date.

12. Governing Law

These Terms are governed by, and shall be construed in accordance with, the laws of Scotland, and the Scottish courts shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising from them.

At checkout, the Hirer must confirm: "I confirm I have read and agree to the BikeBoxHire.io Terms & Conditions, including the damage deposit terms, cancellation windows, and GPS tracker conditions."

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how BikeBoxHire.io collects, uses, and protects personal data in connection with the services described in Part A.

13. Data Controller

BikeBoxHire.io ("we", "us", "our") is the data controller in respect of the personal data described in this Policy. We can be contacted at hello@bikeboxhire.io.

14. Data We Collect

  • Identity and contact data: name, email address, and phone number.
  • Booking data: Box type, hire dates, pickup and return location, and booking reference.
  • Payment data: processed and stored by Stripe on our behalf; we do not store full card details.
  • Location data: the GPS location of the hired Box, tracked for the duration of the hire.
  • Communications: messages sent via WhatsApp or email, including photographs shared for damage assessment.
  • Website usage data: standard analytics and cookies, as described in Clause 18.

We do not collect special category data, save where a Hirer voluntarily provides a medical certificate or doctor's note in support of a claim under Clause 7.

16. Data Sharing

We share the minimum personal data necessary with: Stripe (payment and deposit processing); Booqable (booking and inventory management); partner bike shops in Edinburgh and Glasgow and our locker partner (name, booking reference, and pickup/return dates, for the purpose of handover); our GPS tracker provider; and WhatsApp/Meta, where a Hirer communicates with us by that channel. We do not sell personal data. Where any provider processes data outside the UK or EEA, we rely on appropriate safeguards, including the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or equivalent standard contractual clauses.

17. Data Retention

  • Booking and payment records: retained for 6 years following completion of the hire, for accounting and tax purposes.
  • GPS location history: retained only for the duration of the hire plus a short buffer for dispute resolution, and then deleted.
  • Damage case files, including photographs and correspondence: retained for 6 years.
  • Marketing consent records: retained until withdrawn.

18. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to remember preferences and understand visitor usage. Essential cookies are necessary for the site and booking widget to function; non-essential cookies (for example, analytics) are set only with consent, which may be withdrawn at any time through browser settings or any cookie banner presented on the site.

19. Your Rights

Under UK data protection law, a Hirer has the right to: request access to personal data held about them; request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data; request erasure, where we are not required to retain the data for legal or accounting reasons; request restriction of, or object to, certain processing; request data in a portable format; and withdraw consent at any time where consent is the lawful basis relied upon. Requests should be directed to hello@bikeboxhire.io. We will respond within one month.

20. Complaints

Any complaint regarding our handling of personal data should be directed to hello@bikeboxhire.io in the first instance.

21. Security

We apply industry-standard security measures to protect personal data, including secure payment processing via Stripe, under which we never see or store full card details, and access controls on our booking and communication systems.

22. Changes to this Policy

We may amend this Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services or legal obligations. The date at the top of this page indicates the current version. Material changes to how personal data is used will be highlighted before they take effect.
